A fresh, modern website — easier to manage, faster to load, and with online booking built in

Prepared by Brights Ideas for Elm Lodge Kennels

1What we're proposing

Your current website has served you well, but it's been running on the same software for over ten years — software that is no longer updated or supported. That leaves the site exposed to security risks and increasingly expensive to keep running.

We're proposing a fresh website built from the ground up — keeping everything your customers already know and love, while adding new capabilities that will save you time and bring in more bookings.

Everything you have today is carried over. Your existing pages, photos, prices, contact form and content all move to the new site — your customers will recognise it immediately.

2What you'll get on the new site

Here's what changes for the better — for you and for your customers.

📅

Online booking requests

Customers can request a stay directly from your website — you'll receive each one by email and can confirm or decline. No more phone tag.

✍️

Update your own content

Change your prices, add photos, write a news post — all from your phone or laptop, without needing to call a developer.

⚡

Much faster loading

Pages will load significantly faster than today — better for customers on mobile and better for your Google ranking.

🔒

More secure

Built on fully supported, modern software. No exposed admin login for hackers to target. No database that can be breached.

📈

Better Google visibility

Faster sites rank higher on Google. A modern, mobile-friendly site helps more local pet owners find you when searching.

💷

Lower monthly costs

The new platform typically costs a fraction of what your current Windows/IIS hosting setup does — often £0–£40 per month total.

3New: Online booking requests

One of the biggest additions is an online booking request form. Instead of playing phone tag or waiting for an email reply, customers fill in their details, their dog's information, and their preferred dates — and you receive the request immediately.

Every booking request is also saved in your website's content system, so you have a tidy list of all past and pending enquiries in one place — no more searching through your inbox.
